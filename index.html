Welcome to Ritzy Hotel

A new place to stay in the heart of Sefwi

Ritzy Hotel opened its doors in September 2026 in Sefwi Asawinso, the gateway to Sefwi and the Western North Region, in the heart of Ghana’s cocoa country. The hotel has 56 rooms, a restaurant and bar, laundry services and a conference room — built for visitors, business travellers and anyone passing through the region.

Opposite Asawinso Senior High School, the hotel is easy to find and easy to reach. Inside, you will find warm lighting, wooden furniture and comfortable rooms designed for a good night’s rest.

  • Newly built hotel, opened September 2026
  • 56 rooms across three room categories
  • Restaurant and bar open 7AM – 10PM daily
  • Laundry services and a conference room
