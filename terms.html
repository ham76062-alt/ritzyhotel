Booking enquiries

Enquiries made through this website are requests, not confirmed reservations. Your reservation is confirmed only when the hotel confirms it — by phone, WhatsApp or email — after checking availability.

Rates

Room rates shown on this website are the rates published by the hotel: Standard Single GH¢300, Standard Deluxe GH¢450 and Executive Room GH¢600 per night, inclusive of taxes. Rates may change; the hotel will confirm the rate that applies to your stay when it confirms your booking.

Photos

Photographs on this website are of Ritzy Hotel itself. Room photographs are representative of the room category shown; the hotel can confirm the exact room on enquiry.

Availability

This website does not show live availability. A room category shown here may be unavailable for your dates, and the hotel will tell you so when it replies to your enquiry.

Contact

For any question about these terms, contact the hotel at info@ritzyhotelgh.com or on 020 465 1919. Ritzy Hotel is located opposite Asawinso Senior High School, Sefwi Asawinso, Western North Region, Ghana.
