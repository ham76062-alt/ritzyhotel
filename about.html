Our Story

A new hotel for Sefwi and the Western North Region

Ritzy Hotel held its grand opening on Friday 25 September 2026 and was formally commissioned on Friday 2 October 2026, in a ceremony graced by the Western North Regional Minister and the Regional Director of the Ghana Tourism Authority.

The hotel was built to give Sefwi Asawinso — the gateway to Sefwi, in the heart of Ghana’s cocoa country — a comfortable, modern place to stay, whether you are visiting family, doing business in the region, or passing through on the road.

The Ghana Tourism Authority has described the hotel as having the potential to be rated a three-star hotel. It is led by Chief Executive Officer Mr Pious Ohene Abakah and General Manager Mr Cyril Rollins Abakah.

What we offer

Comfort, food and a place to meet

The hotel has 56 rooms in three categories — Standard Single (GH¢300), Standard Deluxe (GH¢450) and Executive Room (GH¢600) per night, taxes included.

Guests can eat at the restaurant and bar, open 7AM to 10PM every day, use the laundry services, and hold meetings in the conference room. The lounge and reception are open to every guest.
